FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Will AI replace my sales team?

No. The system is designed to automate repetitive sales operations while keeping important conversations and decisions with humans.

Can it work with my existing tools?

Yes. The system is designed around your existing workflow and the tools you already use where practical.

Does AI make decisions on its own?

Only within the repeatable rules and workflows defined for the business. Important, sensitive or uncertain situations can be routed for human review.

Can the qualification rules be customized?

Yes. Qualification, priority, follow-up and escalation logic are configured around the business.

Do I need to replace my CRM?

Not necessarily. VINERA can be designed around an existing lead-management setup.

Can you promise more sales?

No. VINERA automates and organizes parts of the sales workflow; business outcomes depend on the business, offer, leads, market and many other factors.

Can I start with only the AI Sales Team?

Yes. That's the recommended starting point. Other AI workforce capabilities can be added later.
